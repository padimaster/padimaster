# Alex Padilla

**Chief Technology Officer at [GEFIN](https://gefin.uk)** · Madrid

I build where privacy-preserving cryptography and governed AI meet regulated markets.

Today that means owning the technical strategy and architecture of a UK-registered advisory group
operating across eight business lines, and taking its shared platform to production under KYC/AML
controls and a compliance function that holds veto power. Before that I founded **MVP Solutions**,
which grew from an MVP accelerator into an end-to-end project-structuring methodology, and I still
serve as president of **BYPROSTECH S.A.S.** and as founder of **ETH Ecuador**.

I led two consecutive Ethereum Foundation cohorts in Ecuador — the PSE Core Program (2024, Cuenca)
and the ZKET Core Program (2025, Quito).

### What I work on

- **Applied cryptography** — zero-knowledge proofs, MPC, FHE, and privacy-preserving identity
- **Governed AI systems** — multi-agent architectures with auditable scoring and human approval gates
- **Regulated-market delivery** — KYC/AML data rooms, multi-entity IAM, change escalation, audit trails
- **Life-cycle methodologies** — I design frameworks, not just software: explicit phases, human gates,
  and mechanical verification, on the principle that the model generating an output must not be the
  sole judge that it is correct. One of them has produced six sealed due-diligence studies.
